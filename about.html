About AMS College

Excellence in Engineering Education Since 1974

Our History

Founded in 1974, Aalim Muhammad Salegh College of Engineering has been at the forefront of technical education in India. Named after the visionary Aalim Muhammad Salegh, our institution has consistently produced engineers who have made significant contributions to various industries and research fields.

Over the past five decades, we have evolved from a small engineering college to a premier institution known for academic excellence, cutting-edge research, and industry partnerships.

Our Mission

To provide quality engineering education that prepares students to become competent professionals, innovative researchers, and responsible citizens who contribute to the advancement of society and technology.

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized center of excellence in engineering education, research, and innovation, fostering sustainable development and technological advancement.

Our Values

  • Innovation and Research
  • Integrity and Ethics
  • Diversity and Inclusion

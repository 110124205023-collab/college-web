Admission Requirements

Undergraduate Programs

  • 12th standard with minimum 60% aggregate
  • Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics as compulsory subjects
  • Valid entrance exam scores (JEE Main, state-level exams)
  • English proficiency certificate

Postgraduate Programs

  • Bachelor's degree in relevant field with minimum 55% aggregate
  • Valid GATE scores for M.Tech programs
  • Work experience preferred for some specializations
  • Research proposal for Ph.D. programs

International Students

  • Equivalent qualification from recognized board/university
  • TOEFL/IELTS scores for English proficiency
  • Valid passport and student visa
  • Medical fitness certificate
